Ronix is a privacy-first AI meeting assistant for macOS with local audio transcription, meeting notes, and built-in voice-to-text dictation. Record conversations without adding a meeting bot, turn recordings into searchable transcripts, and speak instead of typing in your Mac apps.

**This repository contains public information about Ronix. The application source code is proprietary and is not included.**

## What you can do with Ronix

- **Record meetings without a bot.** Capture your microphone and system audio on your Mac for online meetings, or use your microphone for in-person conversations.
- **Transcribe locally.** Turn speech into text on your device after downloading the required transcription models.
- **Dictate into Mac apps.** Use your voice to write messages, emails, documents, and notes.
- **Transcribe audio and video files.** Import interviews, lectures, podcasts, and other recordings, including common formats such as MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, and MOV.
- **Organize meeting notes and transcripts.** Keep conversations searchable and grouped in folders.
- **Use optional AI features.** Generate summaries and ask questions about meetings using your own supported OpenAI or Anthropic API key.
- **Connect Claude through MCP.** Give Claude access to the meetings you choose through Ronix's scoped connector.

## Common workflows

### Meeting transcription and notes

Capture a call, review its transcript, and keep notes alongside the conversation. Ronix captures audio on your Mac rather than sending a meeting bot into the call. The official website describes use with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack huddles.

### Voice dictation

Speak instead of typing when drafting an email, writing a message, or working in a document. Ronix includes dictation alongside its meeting and file transcription tools.

### Audio and video transcription

Import a recording to make spoken content searchable. This is useful for reviewing interviews, studying recorded lectures, and working with podcast or video material. Transcription quality depends on the language and recording conditions.

## Privacy and offline use

Recordings and transcripts are stored on your Mac. Local recording, transcription, and dictation can work offline once the required models are downloaded.

Optional AI summaries and chat send the authorized text to your selected AI provider. Those features require internet access and your own supported API key. Model downloads and online URL imports also require an internet connection. See the [official website](https://heyronix.com/) for current privacy details.
